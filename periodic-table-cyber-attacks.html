Sophistication runs from 1 (automated, cheap, widely available tooling) to 5 (custom capability, typically criminal groups with deep resources or state actors). It's a rough editorial scale, not a formal standard.

References point to MITRE ATT&CK technique IDs (T…), MITRE ATLAS for AI attacks (AML.T…), and CWE weakness IDs for web flaws. Cases come from public reporting and link to the page they were taken from. 48 are incidents in Europe, 11 are European research or disclosures, and 6 use a global case because no well-documented European incident turned up.
